## Deploy an app

The form produces a Deployment with probes and resources, a Service, an Ingress (with a cert-manager certificate) and a HorizontalPodAutoscaler. Preview the YAML, open it in the editor, or deploy directly.

Have a `docker-compose.yml`? Right-click it → **Convert docker-compose to Kubernetes**.

Manifests are validated and completed against the kinds and CRDs of your cluster when the YAML extension by Red Hat is installed.
